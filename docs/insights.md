# Where should HR act first?

SYNTHETIC EDUCATIONAL DATA — monthly history and recruiting are simulated.

## Finding

Investigate overtime workload in Research & Development first: 74/271 overtime employees have Attrition=Yes (27.3%). This prioritizes the number of flagged exits among overtime groups, not just the highest percentage. It is a demonstration recommendation, not evidence about a real employer.

Across the original file, overtime employees have 127/416 attrition labels (30.5%), compared with 110/1054 (10.4%) without overtime. Difference: 20.1 percentage points.

## Recommendation

Review staffing coverage, workload allocation and manager check-ins in the priority group. Validate with real dated records and exit interviews before a small workload-management pilot. Monitor overtime and period attrition against a comparable group; do not promise an improvement percentage. Use early-tenure onboarding support as a second hypothesis where the tenure table shows elevated share.

## Caveats

These are descriptive comparisons, not significance tests or causal estimates. The file is synthetic teaching data with an unspecified observation window. No employee is counted more than once. Role, pay, tenure and other confounders could explain differences. Small groups produce unstable percentages. Generated months, locations and exit reasons are excluded from this evidence. No individual risk scores or employment decisions are produced.
